GoodObsidian — page backdrops

Nine rulings, three paper colours, PDF and missing-PDF pages, dark paper, and the page floating on its desk.

The nine rulings

Each canvas runs the same routine as the ruling table in src/canvas/backdrop.ts, on a 1024×1448 page. Toggle the theme: the chrome changes and the paper does not.

Paper colour is a separate axis

White, cream, yellow — each shown with the same ruling, as GoodNotes separates “Squared Paper” from “Yellow Paper”.

PDF backdrops, present and missing

A PDF page is just a page with a different backdrop: same ink layer, same tools. When the source cannot be resolved the page still exists, the marker says why, and the annotations are still drawn.

Dark paper — the per-notebook override

Ink is not remapped when the paper goes dark. The black stroke stays black and becomes hard to read — deliberately. The user is told their ink may need recolouring rather than having it changed under them.

The page floats on a desk

A portrait page centred on a dark neutral field with generous margins either side (9% of the page width) and a soft shadow — an object on a desk, not a region filling the pane. Pages are discrete, stacked with a 36 px gap, at fixed intrinsic geometry. The view scales the whole stack to fit and never past 1:1; no viewport dimension is ever written back into a stored coordinate, which is the fix for the cross-device ink drift every competing plugin has.
