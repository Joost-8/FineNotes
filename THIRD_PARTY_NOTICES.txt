FineNotes handwriting model 1.0 (models/finenotes-hwr-1.0.bin)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

FineNotes handwriting recogniser (model "ship-big2")
Copyright 2026 Joost (FineNotes)

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see LICENSE). This NOTICE
must travel with every copy of the model file.

The model is a 5x128 bidirectional LSTM with a character language model. It
was trained on the data below. No training sample is contained in the model
file. Changes made to the data are listed per source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Handwriting (pen trajectories)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

BRUSH (Brown University)
  A. Kotani, S. Tellex, J. Tompkin. "Generating Handwriting via Decoupled
  Style Descriptors". ECCV 2020.
  Used with the written permission of the authors (Prof. James Tompkin, on
  behalf of all authors, 7 October 2026) to train this open-source
  recogniser. Also used: synthetic lines spliced from BRUSH writers' ink.

IAM On-Line Handwriting Database (IAM-OnDB)
  M. Liwicki, H. Bunke. "IAM-OnDB - an On-Line English Sentence Database
  Acquired from Handwritten Text on a Whiteboard". ICDAR 2005.
  Used with the written permission of Prof. Marcus Liwicki, who collected
  the database (9 October 2026), for an Apache 2.0 model. The copy used is
  the one distributed with the DeepWriting dataset (E. Aksan, F. Pece,
  O. Hilliges, CHI 2018), including its character segmentation of the
  IAM-OnDB lines, which was used to splice synthetic lines; no part of
  DeepWriting's own data was used. Also used: synthetic lines spliced from
  IAM-OnDB writers' ink.

UNIPEN (Train-R01/V07)
  I. Guyon, L. Schomaker, R. Plamondon, M. Liberman, S. Janet. "UNIPEN
  project of on-line data exchange and recognizer benchmarks". ICPR 1994.
  Zenodo record 1195803.
  Used under its conditions of use, which allow training recognisers for
  commercial use and require the UNIPEN logo in the product documentation.
  [UNIPEN LOGO]
  No writer or donor identity is referenced. Also used: lines joined from
  UNIPEN words, and lines of UNIPEN characters.

PluColl - The UNIPEN/NICI/HP data collection of 1994
  L. Schomaker. Zenodo record 7894283. Licence: CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changes: the 11 writers who are UNIPEN's "nic" contributor were left out;
  word labels were relabelled to the case each word was written in, and
  unreadable words were dropped (plucoll_audit.py); words were also joined
  into lines.

UJIpenchars2
  F. Prat, M. J. Castro, D. Llorens, A. Marzal, J. M. Vilar. "The UJIpenchars
  Database: a Pen-Based Database of Isolated Handwritten Characters"
  (LREC 2008; version 2). UCI Machine Learning Repository, dataset 177.
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changes: isolated characters were spliced into synthetic lines.
