- **In the guide:** `keyboardShortcuts`, `settingShortcuts`, `moreShortcuts`, `toolKeys`

### Space to move

- **Id:** `spaceDrag`
- **What:** On a keyboard, hold Space and drag with the pen or mouse to move the page.
- **In the guide:** `handTool`, `spaceDrag`

### Erase with Shift

- **Id:** `shiftErase`
- **What:** Hold Shift while writing to erase, or Ctrl (Cmd on iPad) to lasso, for that one stroke.
- **In the guide:** `eraserTool`, `modifierKeys`

### Alt to zoom

- **Id:** `altZoom`
- **What:** Hold Alt and draw up to zoom in or down to zoom out, about the pen.
- **In the guide:** none

## Other tips and switches

### Text boxes turn

- **Id:** `textTurns`
- **What:** Select a text box and drag the knob below it. It settles on every 45°.
- **In the guide:** none

### Exact line spacing

- **Id:** `lineSpacing`
- **What:** Type any spacing from 2 to 30 mm in the template picker.
- **In the guide:** `changeTemplate`

### Drag to size text boxes

- **Id:** `textDragSize`
- **What:** Off: every new box fits its text as you type.
- **Reach:** A switch in settings: "Drag to size text boxes".
- **Words:** resize width click drag text box
- **In the guide:** `settingTextDragSize`

### Lasso takes what it touches

- **Id:** `lassoTouches`
- **What:** Off: at least half of a stroke must be inside the loop.
- **Reach:** A switch in settings: "Lasso takes what it touches".
- **Words:** select partly
- **In the guide:** `settingLassoTouches`

### Pressure-sensitive pens

- **Id:** `pressure`
- **What:** Fountain and brush pens widen as you press.
- **Reach:** A switch in settings: "Pressure-sensitive pens".
- **Words:** pressure thickness pencil
- **In the guide:** `settingPressure`

### Pencil tilt

- **In the guide:** `settingPencilTilt`
